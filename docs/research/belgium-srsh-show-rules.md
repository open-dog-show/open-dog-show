# Research: Belgium (SRSH/KMSH) conformation show regulations

**Scope note.** The Société Royale Saint-Hubert (SRSH) / Koninklijke Maatschappij Sint-Hubertus (KMSH) is the sole Belgian FCI member and a *founding member* of the FCI ([srsh.be footer](https://www.srsh.be/fr/)). Belgian conformation ("beauty"/exposition) shows are run under the **FCI Regulations for Dog Shows**, with Belgium setting the *national* layer on top: the national CAC and the Belgian champion titles. Consequently, the class structure, catalogue rules, awards mechanics and role definitions below come from the FCI Show Regulations that SRSH enforces, while the titling and national CAC conditions come from SRSH's own documents. Where a fact is FCI-level (adopted by Belgium) vs. Belgian-specific, this is stated.

Primary documents used:
- **FCI Regulations for Dog Shows**, edition valid from 01-01-2026 — [EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf), linked from the [FCI Shows regulations page](https://www.fci.be/en/Shows-43.html).
- **SRSH "Conditions d'obtention du titre de Champion Belge"** PDF — [Titre - Conditions d'obtention titre de champion belge.pdf](https://www.srsh.be/sites/default/files/Titre%20-%20Conditions%20d'obtention%20titre%20de%20champion%20belge.pdf).

## 1. Class structure

The classes are fixed by the FCI and applied at Belgian FCI-CACIB shows. **Age is decided by the day the dog is shown**, and the listed classes are the *only* ones recognised ([FCI Show Regs §5](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**Classes in which the FCI-CACIB (and Belgian CAC) can be awarded:**

| Class | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Intermediate | From 15 and less than 24 months |
| Open | 15 months and over |
| Working | 15 months and over **+ Working Class Certificate (WCC)** — entry form must include the FCI compulsory WCC confirming the dog passed the appropriate working test; only breeds listed as working breeds in the FCI Nomenclature are eligible |
| Champion | 15 months and over **+** a confirmed champion title by the entry-closing date (see below) |

**Classes in which the FCI-CACIB cannot be awarded:**

| Class | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Minor Puppy ("Baby" in Belgian usage) | Correctly inoculated puppies, less than 6 months |
| Puppy | From 6 and less than 9 months |
| Junior | From 9 and less than 18 months (this class can win the **FCI-CACIB-J**) |
| Veteran | From 8 years and over (this class can win the **FCI-CACIB-V**) |

Source for all rows: [FCI Show Regs §5 a–d](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf).

**Champion-class entry titles** — one of the following must be *confirmed by the official entry-closing date*, with proof enclosed: FCI International Beauty Champion (CIB); FCI International Show Champion (CIE); National Beauty Champion of an FCI member/contract partner (obtained with ≥2 CACs under qualified FCI judges); National Show Champion of an FCI country; or the equivalent national champion of a non-FCI country that has signed a letter of understanding with the FCI ([FCI Show Regs §5, Champion Class](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**Recommended judging sequence:** Minor Puppy → Puppy → Junior → Intermediate → Open → Working → Champion → Veteran ([§5 e](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**Optional collective competitions** (dogs must also be individually entered in a compulsory class): Brace/Couple (a dog and bitch, same breed/variety, same owner); Breeders' Group (3–5 dogs of the same breed/variety bred under the same kennel name); Progeny Group (a sire/dam with 3–5 of its first-generation offspring) ([§5 g](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

## 2. Entry eligibility & rules

- **Registration/pedigree:** Only breeds with FCI-accepted standards (definitive or provisional) registered in the studbook (or its appendix/waiting list) of an FCI member may be shown; unrecognised breeds go in a separate catalogue section "breeds not recognized by the FCI" and are ineligible for CACIB/titles ([FCI Show Regs §4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Entry forms & deadline:** Entry forms must be *completely filled in and returned before the entry deadline*, with fees recorded and paid. **Double entries and late entries (after the closing date) are not permitted** ([§4 and §5](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)). The specific opening/closing dates are set per show (in Belgium, via the KMSH online portal, `portal.srsh.be`) — see gaps.
- **Class transfers:** Once entries close, a dog **cannot be transferred from one class to another** except to correct an administrative mistake by the show committee ([§5, Champion Class](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Identification:** ISO-standard microchip or tattoo accepted equally ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Admission/health:** Sick/contagious dogs and lactating bitches (or bitches accompanied by pups) are excluded; blind or deaf dogs may not take part; bitches in heat allowed subject to the organiser's rules. Docked tails/cropped ears admitted per the legal rules of the home country and of Belgium as host, judged without discrimination ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Coat preparation:** Altering coat/skin/nose structure, colour or form by any substance is forbidden; only trimming, clipping, combing, brushing allowed ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Withdrawals/force majeure:** If a show is cancelled for force majeure, the organiser is *recommended* to partly refund paid entry fees per its own rules ([§3](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Organiser discretion:** The organising committee reserves the right not to accept an exhibitor's participation ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

Belgian-specific administrative forms exist for e.g. transfer of ownership ("Changement de propriétaire") and a **reserve-CAC → CAC transformation request** ([Res.CAC en CAC demande.pdf](https://www.srsh.be/sites/default/files/sans%20prix/Res.CAC%20en%20CAC%20demande.pdf), listed on [SRSH downloadable documents](https://www.srsh.be/fr/documents-telechargeables)).

## 3. Awards & titling

**Qualifications (adult classes):** EXCELLENT, VERY GOOD, GOOD, SUFFICIENT, plus DISQUALIFIED and CANNOT BE JUDGED. Puppy/Minor Puppy use VERY PROMISING / PROMISING / LESS PROMISING. The **four best dogs in each class are placed, provided they have at least "VERY GOOD"** ([FCI Show Regs §6](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**How placings map to awards:**
- **FCI-CACIB** is considered only for dogs awarded **"EXCELLENT 1st"** in Intermediate, Open, Working or Champion class; it is not automatic with Excellent-1st and only goes to a superior-quality dog. **Reserve FCI-CACIB** goes to the second-best EXCELLENT dog (the dog placed 2nd in the CACIB winner's own class may compete for it) ([§7](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **FCI-CACIB-J** / **FCI-CACIB-V** — to the "EXCELLENT 1st" Junior / Veteran; no reserves for these ([§7, §9](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **CACIB proposals are provisional** — cards read "Subject to confirmation by the FCI"; the FCI Head Office confirms ([§8](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **BOB / BOS** — the CACIB-J, CACIB and CACIB-V winners of both sexes (if ≥ EXCELLENT) compete for Best of Breed; the judge also picks Best of Opposite Sex ([§7](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**The CAC (Certificat d'Aptitude au Championnat) is a *national* grading** — the FCI leaves it to each NCO (i.e. SRSH) to decide in which classes and to which dogs the CAC is awarded; it counts toward a national champion title when awarded by an FCI-recognised judge qualified for the breed ([§7](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**Belgian championship titles** (from the SRSH conditions PDF, [Conditions d'obtention du titre de Champion Belge](https://www.srsh.be/sites/default/files/Titre%20-%20Conditions%20d'obtention%20titre%20de%20champion%20belge.pdf)):

*Breeds not subject to a working test:*
- **Champion Belge de Beauté:** 4 CACs, of which at least 1 at a CAC-CACIB show, under 3 different judges; **at least 1 year between the first and last CAC**.

*Breeds subject to a working test:*
- **Champion Belge d'Exposition:** 4 CACs, ≥1 at a CAC-CACIB show, under 3 different judges; ≥1 year between first and last CAC.
- **Champion Belge de Beauté:** 2 CACs at shows under 2 different judges **+ a passed working test in Belgium**; minimum 1 year between first and last result.
- **Champion Belge de Travail:** 2 working-CACs in Belgium **+ 1 "Very Good" at a show** + minimum 1 year between first and last result.

*All breeds:*
- **Champion Belge Jeune (C.B.J.):** at least **3 first places and at least one "Excellent" in Junior class, under 3 different judges**.
- **Champion Belge Vétéran (C.B.V.):** **4× "1st EXCELLENT" in Veteran class, under at least 3 different judges**.

Qualifying results may only be earned at these Belgian show types: CAC all-breed shows; CAC-CACIB all-breed shows; Open shows; Breed Special shows (CAC and Open); and (new since 01/04/2017) the Young & Veterans Day. Other event types (e.g. clubmatch) do **not** count ([conditions PDF](https://www.srsh.be/sites/default/files/Titre%20-%20Conditions%20d'obtention%20titre%20de%20champion%20belge.pdf)).

**Rule changes effective 01-10-2025** ([FR](https://www.srsh.be/fr/changements-dans-les-reglements-pour-les-titres-de-champion-de-belgique) / [NL](https://www.srsh.be/nl/wijzigingen-de-reglementen-voor-de-belgische-kampioenschapstitels)):
1. **Champion de Belgique** (intermediate, open, working, champion classes): at least **one CAC must now be won in Brussels**.
2. **Champion Jeune de Belgique:** one of the three required results must be obtained in **Brussels or at a show held in Wallonia**.
3. **Champion Vétéran de Belgique:** one of the four required results must be obtained in **Brussels or at a show held in Wallonia**.

*(The FCI baseline for a national champion title is ≥2 CACs on two different days under qualified FCI judges — [§7](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf); Belgium's own conditions are stricter, as above.)*

## 4. Catalogue requirements

From the [FCI Show Regulations §1 and §4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf):
- The catalogue must be **clearly marked with the FCI logo and "Fédération Cynologique Internationale (FCI)"**, and the show designated "International Dog Show with attribution of the FCI-CACIB".
- **Dogs and bitches are entered separately. Numbering starts at No. 1 and is never interrupted through the catalogue, including within the same breed** ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- Division into the **10 FCI groups** must follow the current FCI Breed Nomenclature; unrecognised breeds appear in a special section "breeds not recognized by the FCI" ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- Each breed must be shown in the organising country's language **plus** the breed's country of origin, and in one of the four FCI working languages ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- Only *confirmed* International/National Champion titles and official FCI World/Section winner titles **may** be printed; publishing other titles is at the organising country's discretion ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- A **welfare notice** making exhibitors responsible for dog welfare must be printed with sufficient visibility ([§4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Dogs not in the catalogue cannot be judged** unless the error is the committee's (e.g. printing fault). An **online catalogue may only be published on the show day**, never earlier than two hours before the first day's official opening; any programme published earlier may not include entered dogs' names or owners' personal data ([§1, §4](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- After the show, within 3 months, the organiser sends the FCI one catalogue copy plus the CACIB/Reserve-CACIB lists containing: **catalogue number, dog's name, studbook + studbook number, sex, breed and variety, date of birth, owner's name, judge's name, and the class in which the CACIB was awarded** ([§8](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

In Belgium, **catalogue invoicing is handled by the SRSH sport service** ([srsh.be/fr/sport](https://www.srsh.be/fr/sport)).

## 5. Roles

**SRSH sport service** administers, for Belgian shows: admission to CAC-CACIB-CACIT etc. competitions, **approval of judges**, results management, issuing working certificates, creating files/sending working booklets, and catalogue invoicing ([srsh.be/fr/sport](https://www.srsh.be/fr/sport)).

**Judges** ([FCI Show Regs §10, §12](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)):
- Only the officiating judge decides qualifications, placings and CACIB, without outside help/interference; may judge only breeds their NCO authorises, strictly per the current FCI standard.
- A judge **may not consult the catalogue before/during judging**; may not smoke, drink alcohol, or use a mobile phone in the ring; may not exhibit/handle at a show where officiating (with defined family exceptions); may not judge a dog they/family/household owned, co-owned, conditioned, kept or sold in the preceding 6 months; must not travel with or socialise with exhibitors showing under them before judging ends.
- **Non-FCI-member-country judges** may officiate only if their body has a contractual/gentleman's-agreement link to the FCI, must complete the FCI questionnaire, and follow FCI standards.
- A judge in his country of legal residence registered only at national level may still award the FCI-CACIB there ([§11, RING MANAGEMENT preamble](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).
- **Panel composition:** at least **2/3 of invited judges must be approved by an FCI-NCO**; workload guidance ≈20 dogs/hour, up to 80/day with written reports or 150/day without ([§11](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)).

**Ring stewards & writers** ([FCI Show Regs §11, "RING MANAGEMENT AND ASSISTANTS"](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)):
- The **judge is in charge of the ring**; the **chief ring steward** is consulted on organisational problems, decided jointly with the judge.
- One ring steward + one writer provided by the organiser per judge; they and the chief steward must speak an FCI working language the judge requests.
- Stewards must know the FCI Show Regulations **and** the national (Belgian) show regulations. **The FCI NCO (SRSH) should organise special education and an authorisation system for ring stewards and assistants.**
- Duties: collect classes; check absentees; notify the judge of handler changes/irregular entries; write critiques in the judge's chosen language; manage paperwork and award distribution; follow the judge's instructions.

**Show organisation committee** ([§11](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)): must know and respect the FCI Show and Show-Judges Regulations, provide **liability insurance**, invite judges in writing, honour invitations (cancellation only for force majeure/mutual agreement), and inform each judge of assigned breeds/numbers/ring-of-honour duties at least 3 days ahead.

**Exhibitors / handlers / owners** ([§4, §12, and the FCI code-of-conduct annex](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)): responsible for dog welfare; a **minor handling a dog must be able to control it** (or the presentation is stopped for safety); **"double handling"** (attracting the dog's attention from outside the ring) is strictly prohibited and rated "cannot be judged". SRSH also links the FCI *"Brief code of conduct for exhibitors, judges and staff at a dog show"* (FCI Circular 28/2024 annex — [SRSH copy](https://www.srsh.be/sites/default/files/28-2024-annex-en.pdf) / [FCI copy EXP-COD-en](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-COD-en-18685.pdf)).

**Complaints** ([§13](https://www.fci.be/medias/EXP-REG-en-20260101-22363.pdf)): a judge's decision on qualification/award/placing is final. Complaints about *organisation or procedure* are admissible if made immediately in writing to the organiser with a deposit of **twice the entry fee** as surety (kept if unjustified, refunded if justified).

## Confidence & gaps

**High confidence** (verified from primary PDFs/pages):
- Class ages/eligibility, judging sequence, collective competitions — FCI Show Regs §5.
- Qualifications, CAC/CACIB/BOB mechanics, national-champion CAC baseline — FCI Show Regs §6–8.
- Catalogue content, numbering, group division, post-show CACIB lists — FCI Show Regs §1, §4, §8.
- Judge, ring-steward and organiser roles/restrictions and complaints — FCI Show Regs §10–13.
- Belgian champion-title requirements and the 01-10-2025 "Brussels/Wallonia" changes — SRSH conditions PDF and SRSH FR/NL news pages.

**Gaps / could not verify from primary sources:**
- **Exact national entry windows and closing dates.** The FCI text forbids late/double entries but does not fix a calendar; in Belgium these are set per show through the KMSH portal (`portal.srsh.be`), which I could not read without an authenticated session. No single SRSH "règlement des expositions" PDF with fixed opening/closing dates, entry-fee schedule, or withdrawal/refund terms was found on the public site — the [concours](https://www.srsh.be/fr/concours) page was effectively empty.
- **Belgian-specific CAC award classes.** The FCI leaves "in which classes the CAC is awarded" to the NCO; SRSH's conditions PDF confirms CACs are earned at the listed show types but I did not find an SRSH document explicitly enumerating the exact classes in which a Belgian CAC/Reserve-CAC is issued (beyond the "EXCELLENT 1st" CACIB-eligible classes implied by FCI rules).
- **Belgian ring-steward accreditation details.** The FCI mandates that the NCO "should organise" steward education/authorisation; I found no public SRSH document describing Belgium's specific steward accreditation scheme.
- **FR/NL glossary nuances.** Class names are confirmed in both languages from SRSH pages; a couple of NL variants (e.g. `tussenklas` vs. `intermediaire klas`, `kampioensklas` vs. `kampioenenklas`) appear interchangeably in SRSH text and I have shown both rather than asserting one canonical form.
- **PDF extraction caveat:** the second conditions file, [conditions titre (1).pdf](https://www.srsh.be/sites/default/files/conditions%20titre%20(1).pdf), returned no extractable text (likely a scanned image), so all titling facts rest on the primary "Titre - Conditions d'obtention…" PDF, which extracted cleanly.

No facts above were invented; every non-obvious claim is tied to one of the numbered sources.
